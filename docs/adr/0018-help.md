# ADR 0018 — Help and feedback without a server

**Status:** accepted 2026-11-03.

## Context

- The board «Помощь и отзыв» of the mockups answers common questions and lets
  a person write to the developer, report a bug or suggest an idea.
- Birka has no server yet, and the ledger never leaves the device
  ([ADR 0009](0009-data-model.md)). Whatever help sends has to be what the
  person sees and sends themselves.
- Most people who would write have no GitHub account, and what they write
  about money is private.

## Decision

1. Help lives in `packages/features/help`: `help_common` holds the common
   questions, their search, the letters and `HelpCubit`; each host draws the
   screen and opens the ways out through `HelpLinks`.
2. The common questions and their answers are strings of the app in both
   languages. A search finds a question when every word of it stands in the
   question or in its answer; case and ё do not matter (`searchKey` in
   `l10n`, which the search of operations uses too).
3. A letter opens in the mail app of the person through a `mailto:` address
   to a mailbox of its own, `birka.help@yandex.com` (`supportAddress`). A
   sheet shows all the letter holds before the mail app gets it: a few lines
   to fill in, and the version of the app, its build, the system and the
   language. Operations, amounts and the names of accounts never go into it.
   Without a mail app the address goes to the clipboard, and the screen says
   so.
4. Bugs and ideas can also go to the public issues on GitHub, under a warning
   to leave amounts and the names of accounts out.
5. On the phones `url_launcher` opens the mail app and the browser,
   `package_info_plus` reads the version and `device_info_plus` the system.
6. On the site the letter stands on the page in place of the ways to write,
   as the questions of «Данные» do, and the browser hands its `mailto:`
   address to the mail app. A page cannot learn whether one took it, so the
   letter counts as handed over. The letter names the version of the site,
   the browser with its system from the user agent (`browserSystem`) and the
   language of the browser. Issues open in a new tab.

## Options considered

| Option | Why not |
|---|---|
| A form that sends to our own server | needs the server of sync, its storage and spam protection |
| A hosted widget of support | a third party would read what people write about their money |
| The issues on GitHub alone | most people have no account there, and an issue is public |
| A screenshot or a log in the letter | `mailto:` has no attachments, and a log could hold records |

## Consequences

- Help works offline and keeps "Data Not Collected" in the stores: the app
  itself sends nothing.
- The mailbox has to be read, and its address stays the same: it is in every
  installed app.
- A browser without a mail app does nothing on «Открыть почту». The letter
  shows its address before that, for those who write from webmail.
