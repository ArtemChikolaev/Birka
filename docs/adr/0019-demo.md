# ADR 0019 — The demo in a database of its own

**Status:** accepted 2026-11-03.

## Context

- The board «Данные» has a switch «Демо-данные»: «Пример счетов и операций,
  чтобы осмотреться». A new visitor of the site sees empty screens without it.
- The ledger lives only on the device ([ADR 0009](0009-data-model.md)). A
  demo must not mix with real records or put them at risk.
- Screenshots and checks need the same demo every time.

## Decision

1. The demo lives in a database of its own, `birka-demo`, beside `birka` of
   the ledger. A turn of the switch opens the other one, and the ledger of
   the device stays as it was.
2. The settings of the device keep where the demo stands under `demo`: `off`,
   `fresh` or `on` (`DemoState` in `backup_common`). Turning the demo on
   writes `fresh`. Once its database opens, the host empties it and fills the
   demo (`settleDemo`), then writes `on`, so that each turn starts with the
   same example. Turning it off writes `off`.
3. `fillDemo` writes the demo through `LedgerRepository`, with every rule of
   the ledger. It writes four accounts in rubles, dollars and euros, the
   whole set of categories, and half a year of operations up to now, in the
   language of the app. Those operations are salary and the bills of a
   month, groceries, cafes and roads, savings in dollars and a trip in euros.
   One seed and the same moment give the same demo. No account goes below
   zero.
4. The phones reopen everything on the other ledger. `openLedger` closes the
   database with the objects of the app, registers them again and fills a
   fresh demo, and `BirkaRoot` builds the app anew on the overview. The site
   writes the setting and opens its overview, whose island opens the other
   database. Each database keeps its own lock of the tab.
5. While the demo is on, everything works on it as on the ledger: a copy,
   a restore, the export and the wipe act on the demo.

## Options considered

| Option | Why not |
|---|---|
| Demo records beside the real ones, marked | capital and reports mix the two while the demo is on, and a real operation on a demo account would go with it |
| The demo only on an empty ledger | it cannot be shown on a device that has records, and turning it off would delete what was added on top |
| A fixed file of the demo | its dates would grow old; a generator keeps the last half year |

## Consequences

- Rates for the demo come like rates for the ledger: from the days of its
  operations in dollars and euros, a few dozen days at most.
- A tab of the site that stays open keeps its database until it loads again.
- Nothing on the other screens says that the demo is on; the switch on
  «Данные» does.
