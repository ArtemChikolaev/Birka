# ADR 0027 — Метки and trips

**Status:** accepted 2026-11-12.

## Context

- A category says what money went on, not what it was for. A trip, a
  renovation or a wedding mixes many categories, and people who keep money in
  several currencies want to know what a trip took in all of them at once.
  Other trackers keep adding tags for this.
- The mockups of the first version already had tags on the card of an
  operation and in the search of the operations
  ([docs/design](../design/README.md)).
- A trip abroad mixes currencies: the card of the country on the spot, a
  dollar card for the flat and rubles for the tickets bought weeks before.
- The boards «Метки и поездки» of the canvas draw the phone and the site: the
  chip of the quick add, the picker, the row on the card of an operation, the
  list of метки, the report of a trip and the form of a метка.

## Decision

1. A метка is a row of `tags` (version 11 of the schema): a name and, for a
   trip, its first and last days, both included, and whether the expenses of
   those days get it by themselves (`marks_days`). An archived метка leaves
   the lists and stays on its transactions as they wear it: neither its days,
   nor a change of a transaction, nor a pick, whose pickers do not show it,
   put it on or take it off. Back in the lists, a trip follows its days
   again.
2. A transaction wears its метки through rows of `transaction_tags`, one per
   метка, each with how it came there (`TagMark`): by hand, by the days of
   its trip, or taken off by the person (`off`). Both tables are synced rows
   with tombstones and the outbox, as the rest of the ledger
   ([ADR 0009](0009-data-model.md)). A transaction that goes takes its метки
   along, and so do the transactions of an undone import.
3. The days of a trip that marks them put it on an expense of those days, and
   on nothing else: not on an income, a transfer or an adjustment, and not on
   what a regular payment gave, since the rent at home is not a cost of the
   trip. The tickets and the flat bought before the days get the метка by
   hand.
4. Who decides, in `LedgerRepository`:
   - A new transaction whose draft names no метки gets the trips whose days
     cover it. A draft can carry the метки that the person picked
     (`TransactionDraft.withTags`), and the transaction then wears exactly
     those. A trip whose days cover it and that the person did not pick
     stays on it as `off`, so that its days never put it back.
   - A changed transaction keeps what the person put on and took off, and the
     days follow its new day and kind. `setTags` changes the метки of a
     transaction without changing the rest of it.
   - Saving a trip puts it on the expenses of its days that do not wear it
     yet, and takes it off those that its days put it on and no longer
     cover, or all of them when it stops marking; what the person put on by
     hand stays. The result says how many it was put on, and `unmarkDays`
     takes them back and makes the trip stop marking: the «Вернуть» of the
     toast after a trip added for days that have passed.
   - A transaction whose метки change gets a new stamp, so that whatever
     watches it reads them again.
5. A copy of the ledger carries the метки and how each transaction wears them
   (version 8 of the format, [ADR 0016](0016-ledger-copy.md)). Copies of the
   versions before read with no метки.

## Consequences

- Every write of a transaction reads the trips that mark their days, which
  are a few rows.
- A trip whose days change writes a row for each expense it marks or leaves,
  and stamps those transactions.
- The screens pick метки through the draft and `setTags`; they come in the
  changes after this one and add their points here.
