# ADR 0028 — «Спросить»: questions in words, answered on the device

**Status:** accepted 2026-11-13.

## Context

- People want to ask their money in words: «сколько ушло на кафе в октябре»,
  «траты в поездке», «больше 5000 в этом месяце». Competitors sell this as an
  AI chat over data that already lies on their servers.
- Birka keeps the ledger on the device and sends nothing anywhere. A model
  behind a server would need the Go relay, a place for it to live and the
  person's consent. A prompt cannot keep a model on topic; only an answer in a
  fixed shape, checked by code, can.
- Most questions about money name the same few things: which money, which
  days, which category, метка or account, which amounts, and what is wanted
  most. That fits a small typed query, which a parser without a model can
  fill for the questions it knows. A model can fill the same query later.
- The boards «Спросить» of the canvas draw the phone and the site:
  - the way in from the overview;
  - the page with examples and the questions asked last;
  - an answer with plaques of what the question was read as;
  - the sheet of periods;
  - the facts, the help, the screens and the fixed replies;
  - the operations that an answer leads to.

## Decision

1. The feature `ask` reads a question in words as one typed answer,
   `AskReading`. Nothing leaves the device, and there is no model.
   - `AskData` is a question about money: an `AskQuery` that the ledger
     counts.
   - `AskFactReading` is a fact Birka keeps: what can be spent today, the
     next payments, the capital, the rate of a currency.
   - `AskHelpReading` points to the common questions of the help.
   - `AskPlacesReading` points to the screens it names.
   - `AskRefused` is the fixed reply to advice (investments, rates to come)
     and to what is not about money.
   - `AskUnknown` is a question it did not understand.
2. An `AskQuery` holds:
   - which money (expenses or income);
   - the days (`AskPeriod`);
   - the categories, a метка, an account;
   - words that the note of an operation has to hold;
   - bounds of the amount;
   - what the question wants most: the total, the count, the mean, the
     biggest operations or the categories.

   It turns into plain JSON. A model in a later phase has to fill this same
   shape, and code checks it.
3. The parser, `AskParser`, reads Russian and, more plainly, English.
   - **Periods.** It knows today and yesterday, this and last week, month and
     year, a named month with or without a year, `за 30 дней`, `за 3 месяца`,
     ranges such as `с 1 по 15 октября` and `до 10 ноября`, and dates such as
     `8 ноября` and `08.11`.
   - **Months and comparisons.** Months start on the day the person picked
     (ADR 0024). A month not yet come this year means the one of last year.
     A period stops at today, and the stretch it is set against takes the
     same days: the current month is set against the same days of the month
     before.
   - **Default period.** Without a period a question counts this month; with
     a метка it counts every day of the метка.
   - **Amounts** follow `больше`, `дороже`, `от`, `до` and their kin, with
     `тыс` and a currency, in the currency of the totals by default.
   - **Names.** Categories, метки and accounts are found by their names in
     any form of the word: Russian changes the end of a word, so a word is
     the same when it shares all but its last few letters. An account needs
     a word such as `карта` or `наличные` beside its name.
   - **Words.** What the parser cannot place stays as words of the note.
4. A set of golden questions with exactly the JSON they read as tests the
   parser. A new kind of question adds its questions there first.
5. `answerOf` counts an `AskQuery` over the transactions of the ledger:
   - expenses or income, never transfers or what is only planned;
   - a subcategory counts for its category;
   - every amount converts at the rate of its own day, as the reports do
     (ADR 0012), and what has no rate stays out of the total with a note;
   - beside the total come the count, the mean, the stretch before, bars of
     days, weeks, months or years, the biggest operations and the shares of
     the categories.
6. **Facts.**
   - What can be spent today and the next payments belong to the regular
     payments (ADR 0023). The host puts their card into the answer, and the
     feature does not count them again.
   - The capital and the rates come from the core.
7. The screens of `AskPlace` are named by the feature; the host knows their
   paths.
8. The questions asked last, up to five, live with the settings of the device
   under `ask.recent`, and «Очистить» forgets them. A question joins them
   when the person sends it, takes an example or opens a result.
9. Nothing calls the answers AI, since no model makes them. The caption says
   that the app counted them on the device.
10. `HelpQuestion` and `helpQuestions` move from the help feature to the core
    strings, since a second feature reads them (CLAUDE.md, Architecture).
11. **The phone.**
    - «Спросить» opens from a glass magnifier, first in the header of the
      overview. It is a page pushed over the tabs, with the field in its
      header.
    - Before a question it offers examples built from the ledger: a category
      of the last month, the latest trip or the big spending of the month,
      what can be spent today, the next payment and a question of the help.
      The questions asked last come after them.
    - A typed question is read once the fingers pause. An example or a
      recent question is typed in letter by letter, then asked.
    - An answer about money shows white plaques of what was read, the total
      that rolls (AmountText), a change pill against the stretch before,
      bars and the biggest operations or all of them for a bound or words.
    - The plaque of the days opens a `ChoiceSheet` of periods: this month,
      the two before, the last three whole months and this year.
12. **The operations of an answer.** «Показать N операций» opens the list of
    operations focused on the ids the answer counted (`OperationsFocus`).
    - A plaque under the filters says what they are, and its cross takes it
      off: the list then shows the month of the last of them.
    - «К ответу» leads back, and the month chip waits while the plaque is
      on.

## Consequences

- The parser knows only what its golden set holds. A question it reads
  wrongly shows its plaques, and the person sees the mistake. The fix is a
  new golden question and a rule, never a guess hidden in the answer.
- An opt-in model can come with the relay. It will fill `AskQuery` from a
  question, the language and the date alone, and never see an amount.
- The site comes in the next change and adds its points here.
- Not drawn yet:
  - a sheet for the plaques other than the period;
  - a custom period;
  - synonyms of the categories;
  - proactive hints;
  - the questions asked last in the settings.
