# Glass

GlassSurface is the Liquid Glass of every card, panel and sheet: the backdrop blurred by `blur-glass` with its colors made richer by `saturation-glass`, a gradient from `color-glass-fill-top` to `color-glass-fill-bottom`, a `color-glass-border` hairline, a `color-glass-highlight` line along the top edge and `shadow-glass` under it. Corners are `radius-lg`, padding `space-md`.

Glass needs something behind it: the black `color-background` with the `color-ambient` glow in the top left and bottom right corners. On a flat ground it reads as a plain dark card. Do not put glass on glass. The consumer provides the content.
