The first release: manual tracking of money across currencies, banks and
countries, on Android and on the [site](https://artemchikolaev.github.io/Birka/).

## What it does

- **Accounts** in any currency: cash, cards, savings, deposits and loans, each
  with a card of its own and its balance.
- **Operations:** expenses, income and transfers, between currencies too, with
  categories and notes. Quick add records an expense in three taps.
- **Categories** with subcategories, from a ready set or your own.
- **Overview:** the capital in a base currency at the rates of the Bank of
  Russia, its chart, the accounts and the spending of the month.
- **Operations and reports** by month, with search, filters and categories.
- **Data:** a copy of all records in a file that the site and the app both
  open, its restore, the operations as CSV for a spreadsheet, and the wipe.
- **Demo data** in a database of its own, to look around without touching
  your records.
- **Help and feedback:** common questions and a letter to the developer from
  your own mail app.

Your records stay on the device. Birka has no server, asks for no bank
password and sends nothing but the requests for exchange rates.

## Install on Android

1. Download `birka-v0.1.0.apk` below on a phone with Android 7.0 or later and
   an arm64 processor, which almost every phone of the last years has.
2. Open it and allow installs from your browser when Android asks.

Later releases install over this one and keep your records. To move to
another phone, save a copy in More → Data and restore it there.
