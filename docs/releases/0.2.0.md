The second release: statements of banks instead of typing every operation,
an inbox to sort what they bring, and the money ahead on Android: regular
payments, what you can spend today and reminders. The import and the inbox
work on the [site](https://artemchikolaev.github.io/Birka/) too.

## What is new

- **Import of statements.** CSV and Excel of banks and Zenmoney, and the PDF
  of Sberbank, read on the device. Birka knows the bank by its columns and
  the account by the number of its card; a format it does not know needs its
  columns set once. Operations you already recorded stay out as copies, rows
  that did not read or that the bank declined are listed apart, and an import
  can be taken back out.
- **Inbox.** Operations without a category wait in «Входящие», with hints
  from your past choices. The categories of a bank are placed into yours once
  and kept for its next statements, and a new category can be made right
  there.
- **Regular payments** (Android): rent, subscriptions or a salary, every
  week, month or year. Each one posts itself or waits for «Провести» or
  «Пропустить», and a statement that brings it marks it done with no copy. A
  salary can come in two parts, the advance and the rest, and the advance can
  have a sum of its own. An operation becomes a regular payment from its
  card in one tap.
- **What you can spend today** (Android): the money of the accounts you
  count, until the salary or the end of the month, less the regular payments
  and what you keep aside, split between the days left.
- **Month start** (Android): a month can start on any day from the 1st to
  the 28th, such as your payday, and the overview and the reports follow it.
- **Reminders** (Android): a notification on the day of a payment or the day
  before, and a calendar «Birka» of its own in the calendar of the phone.
- **Notices:** a bell on the overview keeps what Birka told you: the
  reminders and the payments it posted on Android, and the imports on both.
- The More tab ends with the version, the source code and the rates.

Your records stay on the device. Birka has no server, asks for no bank
password and sends nothing but the requests for exchange rates.

## Install on Android

1. Download `birka-v0.2.0.apk` below on a phone with Android 7.0 or later and
   an arm64 processor, which almost every phone of the last years has.
2. Open it and allow installs from your browser when Android asks.

0.1.0 was a test build too, so this one does not install over it: save a
copy in More → Data first, remove the old app, install this one and restore
the copy.

## Not yet
